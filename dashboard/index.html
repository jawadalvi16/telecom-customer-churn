What the business should do

  • Convert month-to-month customers. They churn at 43% versus 3% on two-year contracts — incentivising longer contracts is the single biggest lever.
  • Protect customers in their first year. Churned customers averaged 18 months of tenure versus 38 for those who stayed.
  • Bundle tech support and online security, especially for fiber-optic and higher-paying customers, who churn noticeably more.
  • Target retention spend. Use the model to focus offers on flagged high-risk customers instead of discounting the whole base.
